# Vestas-DCF-Valuation
DCF Valuation and Investment Memo on Vestas Wind Systems

## Overview 
This project presents a full Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) valuation of Vestas Wind Systems, based on publicly available financial data and forward-looking assumptions. The analysis is supported by an integrated financial model and a structured investment memo.

## Valuation Methodology 
The company has been valued using a standard unlevered DCF approach:

- Forecast period: 2026-2029
- Free Cash Flow calculated as: NOPAT + D&A - Capex - ∆NWC
- Discount rate (WACC): 8.0%
- Terminal Growth rate: 2.0%
- Terminal value calculated using the Gordon Growth Model 

## Key Assumptions
The model is driven by operational and financial assumptions derived from historical performance and industry expectations:

- Revenue growth driven by increase in MW deliveries and stable pricing
- Gradual margin expansion reflecting operational efficiency 
- Capex modeled as a percentage of revenue, decreasing over time 
- Stable tax rate at 25%
- Constant capital structure 

## Valuation Output
- Enterprise Value: €15,120m
- Net Debt (2025): €1,174m
- Equity Value: €13,946m

## Sensitivity Analysis
A sensitivity analysis was performed on WACC (7.0%-9.0%) and Terminal Growth rate (1.0%-3.0%). Results show that valuation is most sensitive to changes in WACC, with higher discount rates significantly reducing equity value, while variations in Terminal Growth have a secondary but still relevant impact.

## Key Insights 
The valuation highlights that the majority of Vestas' value is driven by Terminal Value assumptions, reflecting the long-term nature of its business model. This makes the investment case èarticularly sensitive to discount rate assumptions and long-term growth expectations.
